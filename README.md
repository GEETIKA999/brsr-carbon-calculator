# BRSR Carbon Footprint Calculator

Free tool for Indian companies to calculate Scope 1 and 
Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions for BRSR Section C reporting.

## Live App
https://brsr-carbon-calculator.streamlit.app/

## What it does
- Calculates Scope 1 direct emissions from diesel, petrol, 
  LPG, PNG, coal, and furnace oil
- Calculates Scope 2 indirect emissions from purchased electricity
- Shows emission intensity per crore revenue and per employee
- Maps results to BRSR Section C Principle 6 filing requirements
- Downloadable results as text file

## Methodology
- GHG Protocol Corporate Standard 2015
- CEA 2023 India grid emission factor — 0.82 kg CO2e per kWh
- IPCC AR6 fuel emission factors
